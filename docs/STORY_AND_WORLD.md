# Mahery: Story and World

## Logline
Exiled after his father is killed for an act of mercy, a boy named Mahery bonds with a wild animal spirit, earns a place in a new tribe, then returns to face the chief responsible and decide what kind of leader he'll become.

## Mahery
- Around 16 years old.
- His family was already cast out of their pack before the story starts. The reason: his father showed mercy to a blood oath enemy instead of killing him. The clan chief called that mercy a betrayal of the code and killed his father for it. Reveal this gradually through flashbacks, and hold back the "the chief did it" detail for later, once the player already cares about Mahery.
- The story opens the morning he wakes bonded, alone, with no pack and no explanation for why the bond chose him now.
- His personality bends toward his bonded animal. Bear makes him proud, steady, and imposing. Full physical and personality notes for all 11 animals live in SKILLS_AND_CLASSES.md.

Worth keeping even if you change other details: putting the bond right after the exile instead of before makes it feel earned. He has nothing left, and the bond is the one thing that showed up for him anyway.

## The Bond
- Suggestion: bonding is rare and sacred in this world, not something every person gets. That is what makes Mahery's bond suspicious to packs who remember his father, and precious to the ones who don't.
- The player picks the animal at game start: bear, moose, boar, wolf, elk, platypus, mountain lion, bobcat, buffalo, eagle, or falcon.
- The animal talks and has opinions about Mahery's choices and the packs he meets. Write it as a real character, not a narrator.

## Antagonist Faction: the Broken Pack
A suggested name for the group bonded to snake, spider, crocodile, vulture, and hawk.

Why it works: these are also exiles. Faced with the same choice Mahery has, they chose power and cruelty instead of trying to belong again. They are his dark mirror, not just a monster roster. Rename it if you want, but keep the "also outcasts, chose differently" idea if you can, it gives the enemies a reason to exist beyond blocking the road.

**Enemy design notes** (flavor for encounters, not full skill trees like the player animals)
- Snake bonded: poison, ambush, high evasion
- Spider bonded: control the battlefield, immobilize, drain
- Crocodile bonded: high Health, lock a single target down
- Vulture bonded: pick off already wounded targets, curse or debuff
- Hawk bonded: high Speed, strike first, then retreat before engaging again

## The Old Chief
Leader of Mahery's birth clan. Enforced the blood oath code and killed Mahery's father for breaking it. He is the reason the family was exiled. He is a separate thread from the Broken Pack, this is personal history, not the road's monster roster.

Suggestion: give him his own bond rather than reusing a player animal outright, something that reads as old, established power. A Direwolf, an older and larger wolf, fits a clan leader and keeps "pack" as a concept in the room for his fight. Open detail, change it if you land on something better.

## Story Arc

**Act 1, The Road**
- **Prologue**: Mahery wakes bonded, learns the basics, decides to look for a new pack
- **Chapter 1, Snake**: first hostile territory, ends in a snake bonded boss
- **Chapter 2, Spider**: harder terrain, a webbed forest works well, spider bonded boss
- **Chapter 3, Crocodile**: a river or swamp crossing, crocodile bonded boss
- **Chapter 4, Vulture**: Mahery is at his weakest here, a loss or a betrayal, and the vulture bonded boss preys on that
- **Chapter 5, Hawk**: fastest and hardest fight of the road, leads into the Broken Pack's leader
- **Act 1 Finale**: defeat the Broken Pack's leader, earn a place in a new tribe

**Act 2, The Reckoning**
- Land the full truth in a late flashback, once the player already cares about Mahery: the old chief killed his father.
- With a tribe behind him now, Mahery chooses to go back. Not out of desperation this time, out of his own will. That's what makes the final choice mean something, he isn't acting from having nothing left to lose anymore.
- **Final Chapter**: return to the birth clan, boss fight against the old chief
- **The Choice**: kill the old chief or show mercy and banish him. See The Ending below.

## The Ending
The choice after the old chief's boss fight:

- **Kill him.** Mahery takes both clans by right of conquest and blood. Fits a chief who enforced his code through killing. Sequel hook: the old chief's son survived and is old enough to want revenge of his own, a new blood feud starts with Mahery on the other side of it this time.
- **Banish him.** Mahery takes both clans by choosing the same thing that got his father killed, mercy, and proving his father right. Sequel hook: the old chief is alive somewhere, cast out the way Mahery once was. He could come back.

Either choice ends with Mahery as head of both clans. The difference is what kind of leader that makes him, and what shows up if you build a sequel. Keep both epilogues short, a few lines each is enough, the hook is what matters, not a full resolution.

This is also where the blood oath theme pays off. His father died for choosing mercy. The old chief killed for enforcing a code. Mahery gets his father's exact fork in the road, except this time he holds the power instead of being at someone else's mercy.

## Open Design Decision
**Is bonding common or rare in this world?** Changes how strangers react to him in every chapter.
