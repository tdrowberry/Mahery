# Mahery: Skills and Classes

## Design Rule
Every animal shares the same 4 skill types with the same numbers, just a different name and flavor text. That is what makes "bear slash and eagle claw are the same move" work, and it means those 4 skills only need to be balanced once for all 11 animals. Each animal also gets 1 signature skill nothing else has, grounded in a real trait of that animal.

This isn't just a moveset choice. The bond changes Mahery physically and mentally toward whichever animal he picked. Build the traits below into his character art and his dialogue and combat lines.

## Physical and Personality Traits

| Animal | Physical Traits | Personality |
|---|---|---|
| Bear | Broad frame, coarse dark hair on the arms, blunt claws | Proud, steady, slow to anger, immovable once he digs in |
| Moose | A heavily built frame, broad shoulders, patches of tough, velvety skin on the forearms | Blunt, forceful, protective, hard to move once he plants himself |
| Boar | Bristled hair on the forearms, slightly longer lower canines | Brash, quick to anger, reckless, never backs down |
| Wolf | Sharper canines, a keen sense of smell, a gray streak in his hair | Loyal, reads people fast, restless when he's alone |
| Elk | Lean and tall, sharper hearing, a faint ridged brow | Composed, a natural leader, quietly dignified |
| Platypus | Slightly webbed fingers, smoother and more sensitive skin on the hands | Calm under pressure, dry humor, comfortable being underestimated |
| Mountain Lion | A lighter step, tawny patches at the temples, sharper canines | Patient, solitary, calculating, strikes only when sure |
| Bobcat | Quick reflexes, flecked eyes, sharpened nails | Independent, prickly, quick witted, slow to trust |
| Buffalo | A heavyset frame, coarse dark hair across the shoulders | Unshakeable, blunt, dependable, slow to start and hard to stop |
| Eagle | Unusually sharp eyesight, nails hardened like talons | Sharp, focused, sees the big picture, impatient with details |
| Falcon | Narrow build, keen distance eyesight, fast reflexes | Restless, fast talking, thrives on momentum |

Heads up: 11 distinct personalities means 11 versions of how Mahery reacts to the same story beats if you take this all the way. That's a real writing job, not just a data table. For Phase 1, write Bear's voice fully and leave the other 10 as data only, traits listed, dialogue not yet written, until the story itself gets built.

## Stats Recap
Vitality (Health), Strength (physical damage), Instinct (special effects and Spirit), Speed (turn order, crit and evasion). Full formulas are in GDD.md.

## Rank Up
Every skill ranks up 3 times as Ability Points are spent, matching Sonny 2's pattern of showing the current rank and a preview of the next one. Shared skills rank identically across all 11 animals. Unique skills rank on their own curve, since there's no other animal's version to compare against.

## Balancing Note
Treat every number above as a first draft, not a tested value. Because the 4 shared skills are one formula used 11 times, tuning that formula once improves every animal's basic kit at the same time. Spend real playtesting time on the 11 unique skills individually, since that is where actual balance problems will turn up.
